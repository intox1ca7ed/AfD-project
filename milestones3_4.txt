# milestones3_4.txt

This note describes the next stages after Milestone 2 is completed.

Current project state before Milestone 2
- Corpus construction, cleaning, deduplication, QC, post-retrieval consolidation, and refactoring are complete.
- Main monthly media salience is based on manual Nexis monthly total result counts, not kept/downloaded counts.
- Downloaded and kept article counts are diagnostics / text-sample sizes.
- Polling is prepared in two blocks:
  - manual Wahlrecht historical supplement, 2013-09 to 2016-12;
  - DAWUM monthly Bundestag AfD polling, 2017-01 to 2025-12.
- Combined salience-polling panel now covers 2013-09 to 2025-12.
- Descriptive event-window results currently show:
  - Cologne: strong positive AfD polling shift;
  - Chemnitz: weak/ambiguous polling shift;
  - Correctiv: clear negative post-shock polling movement.
- Lag diagnostics are descriptive and modest; no causal claims are made.

Milestone 2, now underway
- Build article-level and monthly text/content indicators from the kept corpus.
- Create dictionary-based indicators for migration/asylum, security/public order, AfD/far-right, remigration/democracy-threat, and negative/threat tone proxy.
- Aggregate these indicators monthly.
- Validate indicator behavior around Cologne, Chemnitz, and Correctiv.

After Milestone 2, do not immediately jump to modelling.
First inspect whether the text indicators are meaningful.

============================================================
Milestone 4 — Final descriptive diagnostics and minimal modelling checks
============================================================

Goal
Use the full analysis panel to create the final empirical diagnostics. Keep this modest and thesis-appropriate.

This stage should answer:
- Are content indicators descriptively associated with AfD support or monthly AfD changes?
- Do different shocks show different indicator patterns?
- Are results robust to using polling mean vs median?
- Are results robust to +/- 3 month and +/- 6 month windows?
- Do lagged indicators show plausible short-run movement?

Recommended notebook
- notebooks/09_final_descriptive_diagnostics.ipynb

Expected inputs
- data/panel/monthly_full_analysis_panel_2013_2025.csv/parquet
- data/panel/event_window_descriptive_summary_combined_2013_2025.csv
- data/panel/lag_diagnostics_descriptive_summary.csv

Expected outputs
- data/panel/final_event_window_text_indicator_summary.csv
- data/panel/final_lag_indicator_summary.csv
- docs/final_empirical_results_summary.md
- figures/09_notebook_figures/*.png

Core analyses

1. Event-window text indicator summaries
For Cologne, Chemnitz, and Correctiv, compute +/- 3 and +/- 6 month pre/post differences for:
- AfD polling support mean;
- media volume salience;
- migration indicator;
- security/public-order indicator;
- AfD/far-right indicator;
- remigration/democracy-threat indicator;
- negative/threat tone proxy.

This should produce a thesis-ready table like:
- shock;
- expected mechanism;
- AfD change +/-3m;
- AfD change +/-6m;
- main text indicator change +/-3m;
- main text indicator change +/-6m;
- interpretation.

2. Lag diagnostics with text indicators
Run descriptive lag correlations for lags 0..6:
- text indicator lag k vs AfD polling level;
- text indicator lag k vs AfD monthly change.

Do this for:
- combined panel;
- historical block;
- DAWUM block.

Important: correlations are descriptive only.

3. Polling mean vs median robustness
Where possible, compare event-window summaries using:
- afd_poll_support_mean;
- afd_poll_support_median.

This is quick and useful because monthly mean can be affected by pollster distribution.

4. Optional simple regression diagnostics
Only if time permits and the descriptive outputs look stable.
Keep regressions minimal and explicitly exploratory.
Possible examples:
- afd_poll_support_change ~ media_salience_lag1
- afd_poll_support_change ~ media_salience_lag1 + security_indicator_lag1
- afd_poll_support_change ~ media_salience_lag1 + remigration_democracy_indicator_lag1

If regressions are added:
- do not overinterpret p-values;
- include only a few variables;
- account for the polling source boundary at 2017 if possible;
- treat as robustness/diagnostic, not causal identification.

Recommended stance on statistical significance
Do not make statistical significance the success criterion.
The project is strongest as a transparent descriptive/event-oriented thesis:
- validated corpus;
- validated monthly salience indicator;
- validated polling panel;
- first text/content indicators;
- shock-specific descriptive patterns;
- carefully stated limitations.
