# Descriptive Event-Window Results

## Data Basis
- Combined monthly panel covering `2013-09` to `2025-12`.
- Media salience is based on Nexis monthly total result counts.
- Polling source blocks:
  - `2013-09` to `2016-12`: manual Wahlrecht historical supplement (Politbarometer, Infratest dimap, GMS).
  - `2017-01` to `2025-12`: DAWUM monthly Bundestag AfD polling.
- Results below are descriptive pre/post comparisons, not causal estimates.

## Main Results Table

| Shock | Shock month | Expected direction | +/-3m AfD change | +/-6m AfD change | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cologne | 2016-01 | increase | +3.98 pp | +6.49 pp | Strong positive shift, but embedded in the broader 2015-2016 refugee-crisis salience wave. |
| Chemnitz | 2018-08 | ambiguous / possibly increase | +0.45 pp | +0.07 pp | Weak/ambiguous; not a clear positive polling shock in simple monthly pre/post comparison. |
| Correctiv | 2024-01 | decrease | -3.28 pp | -3.65 pp | Clear negative post-shock polling movement in descriptive window. |

## Short Interpretation
The three shocks do not show a uniform empirical pattern. Cologne aligns with a strong positive shift in AfD polling support during the broader refugee-crisis media wave. Chemnitz is theoretically relevant but descriptively ambiguous, consistent with a mechanism in which law-and-order salience may have been offset by far-right stigmatization. Correctiv shows the clearest negative post-shock movement, consistent with a legitimacy/remigration/democratic-threat mechanism. These patterns are descriptive and should not be interpreted as causal estimates.

## Caveats
- Monthly aggregation is coarse.
- The combined polling series has a source boundary at `2017-01`.
- Cologne uses the manual Wahlrecht supplement; Chemnitz and Correctiv use DAWUM.
- Broad media volume may not capture framing/tone, especially for Chemnitz and Correctiv.
- Later content/framing indicators could improve mechanism-specific interpretation.
